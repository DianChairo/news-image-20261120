A 1024×1024 editorial front-page infographic that sums up Friday 20 November 2026 in six verified stories. A full-width lead panel covers Trump pushing any decision on new Iran strikes to 2027 — "We'll see what happens after January 1" — and a two-column grid covers Cyclone Akvan's landfall on India's east coast with about 300,000 people evacuated, ex-judge Yashwant Varma telling Parliament his resignation is no admission of guilt and ends his removal proceedings, GTA VI topping an estimated $1.84bn on launch day to pass The Odyssey's lifetime box office, and Anthropic soaring in its record $81.3bn Wall Street debut. A bottom strip closes the page with Glenn Phillips' 118 lifting New Zealand past 300 on day two of the Wellington Test. Everything is drawn in pure Pillow shapes and text — no photos, no network calls — in a midnight navy, gold and cream palette.

`build_image.py` renders the infographic to `entry.png`, and `main.py` copies that file into the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
